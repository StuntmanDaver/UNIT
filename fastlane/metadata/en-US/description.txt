UNIT helps tenants inside commercial properties discover nearby businesses, share community updates, receive property notifications, and manage promotions from one place.

Tenants can browse the building directory, post announcements or events, edit their business profile, and view alerts from property admins.

Property admins can manage tenants, properties, promotion review, pricing tiers, and push broadcasts through admin-only tools. UNIT is built for property-scoped communities, so users see the tenants and updates that belong to their assigned property.
